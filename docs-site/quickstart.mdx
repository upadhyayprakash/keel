The commands below work in the Keel shell (run `keel`) without the prefix, or with `keel` in front in any terminal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Keel">
    ```bash
    keel
    ```
    You're in the Keel shell. It opens on `today`, which is empty for now and suggests the first two commands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a client and a project">
    A client is who the work is for, or any area of your life. A project is one piece of work inside it.
    ```text
    client new acme "Acme Corp"
    project new acme/website "Website rebuild"
    ```
    Keel answers `You are now working on acme/website`. New items land in the project you're working on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture a promise you made">
    ```text
    owe send pricing deck @ravi due:thu
    ```
    `@ravi` is the person (created as you go) and `due:` takes `today`, `tomorrow`, a weekday, `+3d` or a date. Keel answers with a ref: `c1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture a promise made to you">
    ```text
    wait design files @meera due:fri
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick today's focus">
    ```text
    focus c1
    today
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the loop">
    ```text
    done c1
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Press Tab at any point. After `owe send deck` Keel suggests people, then due dates, then where the promise came from.
</Tip>
