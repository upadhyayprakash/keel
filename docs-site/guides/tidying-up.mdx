---
title: "Tidying up"
description: "Rename, archive and delete clients, projects and people."
---

```text
client rename acme "Acme Inc"          # display name
client rename acme slug:acme-inc       # short name; paths become acme-inc/...
set p1 title:"Website v2" slug:site    # a project's name and slug
client archive acme                    # finished with a client: hide it, keep everything
client unarchive acme                  # bring it back
project delete p3                      # a project and its commitments
client delete acme                     # a client with all its projects
people                                 # everyone you've mentioned with @
person rename @ravi "Ravi Kumar"       # show a full name
person merge @rav @ravi                # fix a typo: move everything to @ravi
```

Archived clients and their projects are hidden from lists (`clients --all` and `projects --all` show them). Their open commitments stay in `today` and `ls` until you close them.

<Warning>
Deleting shows what goes with the item and asks you to type its name to confirm. Deleted items disappear from Keel; your daily backups from before still have them. In scripts, `--yes` skips the question, and `--json` without `--yes` never deletes.
</Warning>
