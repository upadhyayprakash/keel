---
title: "Friday review"
description: "A guided walk through every open loop, about 30 minutes."
---

```text
review
```

Keel walks you through six steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Empty the inbox">Turn each item into an owe or a wait, keep it, or drop it.</Step>
  <Step title="Every overdue item">Done, renegotiate, or drop.</Step>
  <Step title="Everything you're waiting on">Nudge, or close it.</Step>
  <Step title="Each active project">Is its resume note still true?</Step>
  <Step title="Next week">One priority per client. They show at the top of `today` all week.</Step>
  <Step title="The week summary">What you kept, renegotiated and slipped, and your time per client.</Step>
</Steps>

From Friday until you've done it, `today` shows a reminder. Weeks start on Monday.

## The summary on its own

```text
summary          # this week
summary day      # today
summary last     # last week
summary +acme    # one client
```

Your **kept rate** counts promises kept on time and renegotiated in time, against those that slipped.
