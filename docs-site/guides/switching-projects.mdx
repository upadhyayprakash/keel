---
title: "Switching projects"
description: "Restart in two minutes instead of thirty."
---

```text
switch northwind/portal
```

Keel asks three short questions about the project you're leaving. Answer in a few words, or press Enter to skip one:

```text
│ Where are you?   pricing page copy half done
│ What's next?     send the deck to Ravi
│ Unsure about?    whether phase 2 is approved
```

Then it shows the resume note you left on `northwind/portal` last time, plus its open items. The prompt and `today` now show you working on `northwind/portal`, and new items land there.

You can also give the answers in one line:

```text
switch acme/website where:"pricing page half done" next:"send deck"
```

## Stopping for the day

```text
park
```

Saves a resume note and leaves you in no project.

## Pausing a project

```text
set p2 status:paused
```

Paused projects drop out of `today` but stay in the weekly review. Your time per client in `summary` comes from your switches.
