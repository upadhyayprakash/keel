<Update label="0.5.0" description="2026-11-16">

Goals, phone login and Lists. Your data migrates by itself the first time you run this version (a daily backup is in `~/.keel/backups`).

### Added

- **Goals** ([ADR 0004](https://github.com/upadhyayprakash/keel/blob/main/docs/adr/0004-goals.md)): set and finish one small goal, with the parts that make it likelier to get done.
  - `keel goal new` asks what done looks like, the deadline, the time you will give it, what could get in the way and an if-then plan, then the steps, each with when and where and a smaller version for a bad day. Anything given inline (`done:`, `due:`, `obstacle:`, `plan:`, `step:`) is not asked, so `--json` works.
  - `goals`, `goal show g1`, `goal step done g1 2`, `goal done g1` and `goal drop g1` move it along, with progress ("1 of 4 done", then "2 left" past halfway) and tab completion for goals and their open steps.
  - Closing a goal asks three short review questions (also inline with `happened:`, `why:`, `change:`). Once your finished goals run late, new goals offer a deadline that allows for it: "Your last 2 goals took 30% longer than planned."
  - Goals show in `today` (with the next step), in the Friday `review`, and in `summary`. `export` and `import` carry them (export version 2), and `export --md` lists them.
  - A Goals phone app at `/goals`, served by `keel serve`, installable from the browser: set a goal, see Today, tick steps, close a goal with its review. When `keel serve` is not reachable it opens with what your phone last saw, marked "Not live", and refuses changes until Keel is back.
  - Reminders at the time you planned, with **Done** and **Do the fallback**, sent as web push by `keel serve` itself (no other service). Reminders it slept through arrive as one catch-up push, and a step still open when its day ends is marked missed. (Turning reminders on from the phone comes in a later release.)
  - A Goals API under `/api/goals` (see Reference, Goals API).
- **Sign in from your phone** to `keel serve` with a passkey (Face ID, a fingerprint or your screen lock): `keel serve --public-url https://<your-tunnel-name>`, then `keel auth passkey add` and scan the QR code. Six-digit authenticator codes work as a fallback (`keel auth totp enable [--label <name>]`). `keel auth`, `keel auth passkey list|remove` and `keel auth sessions [revoke <id>|--all]` manage it, from your own terminal only. `keel serve --host <address>` listens beyond this machine and refuses to start without a login and HTTPS.
- **Lists** ([ADR 0005](https://github.com/upadhyayprakash/keel/blob/main/docs/adr/0005-lists.md)): keep things together under a project, as a plain collection or a tracked checklist.
  - `keel list new "D-mart groceries" --tracked` starts one. `list add l1 item:milk item:eggs`, `list done l1 1 2 4-6` and `list state l1 3 blocked` fill it in (an item is open, doing, blocked or done), and a tracked list shows "4 of 7 done". `list order l1 3 to 1`, `list set l1 sort:state|created|alpha|manual` (changes only how it is shown, never the stored order), `list pin`, `list archive`, `list move l1 +acme/website`, `list delete`.
  - A list you use again, like a weekly shop, resets with `list reset` (it asks first). When every item is done, Keel offers Reset or Archive and never archives on its own.
  - Where it goes: `+client/project` names a project, a client alone uses its General project, and with neither it goes to the project you are working on, or to a Personal client made when first needed. General projects are shelves, not work: they stay out of "going quiet" and the weekly review, and `projects` shows one only once it holds a list.
  - `list` (or `lists`) shows them all, narrowed by `#tag`, `+project` or words. `show l1` and a project's page (`show p3`) show a list too. Lists stay out of `today`.
  - On your phone, `/goals/lists`: cards with covers and search, drag-to-reorder with a finger or the arrow keys, item states, sort, add (paste a whole list), edit, reset, archive, pin, move, delete.
  - Covers: a built-in placeholder (light and dark), or your own SVG or PNG up to 200 KB (`list cover l1 <file> [dark:<file>]`, or on the phone). SVGs are cleaned when saved and only ever shown as images. A Lists API under `/api/lists` (see Reference, Lists API).
- **Claude Code plugin 0.2.0**: `/keel:lists` and `/keel:goals` (Claude asks you for what done looks like, the obstacle and your if-then plan before saving a goal), and `/keel:today` now answers about goals and lists. Claude never resets or deletes a list, or closes a goal, unless you ask. These need Keel 0.5.0 or newer.

### Changed

- `export` writes version 3 (it adds lists and the default-project flag). Version 1 and 2 files still import; an older Keel refuses a newer file instead of dropping data.
- `show` takes goal and list refs (`show g1`, `show l1`), and deleting a client or project deletes its lists too and says how many.
- New dependency: `web-push`, for reminders.

</Update>

<Update label="0.4.0" description="2026-10-17">

### Added

- A Claude Code plugin in `plugins/claude`, installable with `/plugin marketplace add upadhyayprakash/keel`: `/keel:capture` saves what you say (owe, wait, done, new dates, resume notes) through the keel CLI, and `/keel:today` answers what's on. It never deletes and always names the project.
- `@me` for promises to yourself: `owe renew passport @me due:fri` shows as "for myself" instead of "I owe" someone. You can't wait on `@me`, it isn't listed in `people`, and `person merge @yourname @me` moves promises you filed under your own name. If you already had a person with the handle `me`, it now counts as you.

### Fixed

- Dates outside the current year show their year (`Sat 16 Oct 2027`), in lists, `show`, confirmations and history. They used to show only the day and month, so a date next year looked like one this year. `show` no longer repeats the date in brackets when it's more than a week away. Dates follow Keel's clock, not the system clock.

</Update>

<Update label="0.3.0" description="2026-10-13">

### Added

- Use Keel on several devices ([ADR 0001](https://github.com/upadhyayprakash/keel/blob/main/docs/adr/0001-multi-device-sync.md)): `keel db connect <address>` keeps a synced local copy of a hosted libSQL database (Turso or a libSQL server) on each device. The first device copies its data up after a confirmation and a check; later devices adopt it, and local data is backed up rather than mixed. The token is stored in the macOS Keychain or the Linux keyring. Offline, reading works and changes are refused clearly. `keel db` shows where data lives; `keel db disconnect` goes back to a local copy.
- `KEEL_HOME` moves Keel's folder (database, config, backups, history).
- The Keel shell: run `keel` on its own (or `keel shell`) for a session where you type commands without the `keel` prefix. The prompt shows the project you're working on, Tab completes, ↑ recalls history across sessions, questions are asked inline, `exit` or Ctrl+D leaves.
- Autocomplete that knows every command: subcommands after `client`, `project` and `person`; the right clients for `rename`, `archive` and `unarchive`; refs that fit each command (`nudge` only offers what you're waiting on); keys and status values for `set`; and `owe`/`wait` guide you through who, when and where from. Shared by the browser terminal, the shell and zsh/bash completion.

### Fixed

- Faster on a hosted database: opening skips the migration step when nothing is new (it used to cost a write round trip every time), and changes are sent to the hosted database in one request before the local copy catches up. Measured from India to Ireland: `ls` 4.1 s to 0.9 s, a change 4.0 s to 2.5 s (under 1.1 s in the shell); much less with a nearby region.
- A hosted connection dropped while idle no longer loses a change: Keel retries once, safely, since every row's id is set before sending.
- Backup and file names use the local date, not UTC.

</Update>

<Update label="0.2.0" description="2026-10-12">

### Added

- `client rename`, `client archive` / `unarchive` and `client delete`. Archived clients and their projects are hidden from lists (`clients --all` and `projects --all` show them); their open commitments stay visible until closed.
- `project delete`, and `set p7 slug:<new>` to change a project's slug.
- `people`, `person rename @ravi "Ravi Kumar"` and `person merge @rav @ravi`.
- Deleting shows what goes with it and asks you to type the name to confirm. `--yes` skips the question for scripts; `--json` without `--yes` never deletes. Deletes are soft, so backups from before still hold everything.
- Screenshots of the CLI and the browser terminal in the READMEs.

### Changed

- Output says "working on acme/website" instead of "context", and `today` on an empty database shows the first two commands to run.

</Update>

<Update label="0.1.0" description="2026-10-12">

The first release: one command language in your terminal and in the browser.

### Added

- Commitments: `owe` and `wait` with a person, due date (natural language), project, source (meeting, Slack link, ...) and tags. `done`, `drop`, `renegotiate` (agreed new date, counted separately from a silent slip), `nudge` and `set`.
- Inbox: `in` captures anything, `triage` walks it.
- `today`: focus (up to 3, set with `focus`), due and late, waiting-on follow-ups, coming up, quiet projects, this week's priorities and a Friday review reminder.
- Clients and projects with resume notes. `switch` asks for a note on the way out and shows the one for where you're going. `park` saves a note and leaves.
- `meeting` mode: `>` for owe, `<` for waiting, anything else is a note. Confirms before saving.
- Weekly `review` (Friday, six steps) and `summary day|week|last` with the kept rate and time per client.
- `keel serve`: the same terminal in the browser, with autocomplete, a live preview of new commitments, clickable refs and a light, dark or auto theme.
- Light and dark palettes in the CLI. Auto mode asks the terminal for its background color (OSC 11), after `KEEL_THEME` and `COLORFGBG`.
- `--json` on every command: `{ ok, data, text }` or `{ ok: false, error, hint }`, with a non-zero exit code on failure.
- `export` (JSON) and `export --md` (readable snapshot), `import` to restore into an empty database, and a daily automatic backup that keeps the last 14.
- Shell completion for zsh and bash: `keel completion zsh|bash`.
- Storage on libSQL: a local SQLite file by default, ready for a libSQL server or Turso later. CI runs the core tests on a file and on a libSQL server.

</Update>
